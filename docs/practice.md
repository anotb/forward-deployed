# Enterprise AI practice and sources

These skills help a deployment team connect AI capability with a better way for an organization to work. The durable method is to find valuable work, explore what current systems can do, build a consequential first version and use the results to develop the service and the team around it.

The instructions and worked examples are independently authored. The following primary sources inform particular decisions. Product capabilities, availability and economics should be checked at the time of the assignment.

## Where to look for value

- [OpenAI: How frontier firms are pulling ahead](https://openai.com/index/introducing-b2b-signals/) (May 2026, updated August). Enterprise Signals examines deeper, more complex and delegated AI use. It informs the library's focus on the work people entrust to AI and the practices that help successful teams spread it. Usage intensity alone does not establish a customer's return on investment.
- [OpenAI: How workers are unlocking new ways of working](https://openai.com/index/unlocking-new-ways-of-working/) (September 2026). Research on recurring work across occupational boundaries suggests looking at what employees become able to do, including responsibilities and handoffs that can change. This informs discovery and adoption alongside improvements to existing tasks.
- [OpenAI: Introducing Frontier Alliances](https://openai.com/index/frontier-alliance-partners/) (February 2026). Describes enterprise deployment work connecting leadership, workflow redesign, integration and change management. The skills cover that connected assignment, from the customer conversation through useful operation.

## How to turn capability into a service

- [Anthropic: Building effective agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/building-effective-agents) (December 2024, subsequently updated). Distinguishes fixed workflows from model-directed work and explains when decomposition, routing or parallel work helps. Use task structure to choose the arrangement.
- [Anthropic: Scaling Managed Agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/managed-agents) (April 2026). Separates durable session state, the agent loop and execution environments. It also shows why workarounds for an older model should be reconsidered when capability improves. This informs both runtime selection and upgrade design.
- [Anthropic: Effective context engineering for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-context-engineering-for-ai-agents) (September 2025). Treats instructions, tools, retrieved material and continuing state as a joint design problem. This informs selective access to business context and testing what information the agent needs at each decision.
- [Anthropic: Effective harnesses for long-running agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/effective-harnesses-for-long-running-agents) (November 2025). Examines progress across sessions and checking completed work in its environment. Its coding examples inform recovery and completion design for long tasks; the appropriate mechanics depend on the workload.
- [Anthropic: Demystifying evals for AI agents](https://www.anthropic.com/engineering/demystifying-evals-for-ai-agents) (January 2026). Evaluates the model and harness together, checks the resulting environment and separates evaluation defects from agent defects. This informs outcome checks, trace diagnosis and testing both productive action and correct restraint.
- [OpenAI: Rethinking skills and prompts for GPT-6 Astra](https://developers.openai.com/blog/rethinking-skills-and-prompts-for-gpt-6-astra). Argues for concise skill selection, useful context, decision boundaries and fewer rigid recipes. This informs the library's instruction design. Individual assignments still determine the model and tools to use.

## Using the worked examples

Each example develops a deployment decision from specific inputs: an AI job to delegate, a technical choice, an operating change or an investment. They show which facts change the recommendation. Case details and numbers are fictional teaching material, identified in the reference; they are not customer results or suggested default targets.

Use a relevant example to sharpen reasoning, then return to the user's organization, environment and intended result. Source checks for this revision were completed in October 2026.
