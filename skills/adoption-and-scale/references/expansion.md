# Expand a useful capability

Look for where the installed capability can create the next valuable outcome. Broad AI adoption may spread through trusted colleagues, shared work products, role communities, and manager-supported practice. A deployed service may deepen the same workflow, add an adjacent role, widen a customer segment, extend operating hours, enable a paid service, or support another account. Choose the next expansion because of demand, value, and transferable capability, rather than proximity on the organization chart.

Compare what stays stable with what changes. Similar terminology can conceal different legal objects, policies, customer promises, access rules, source quality, and expert practices. Existing reasoning and case lifecycle may transfer while local thresholds and contracts must change. Determine what the next deployment can reuse, what must be adapted, and what must be proven anew.

Test the next binding constraint before extending the commitment. Higher volume can expose review saturation, slow sources, expensive refreshes, or support coverage. A new segment can expose a missing domain concept even at low volume. Another account can require a different identity and operating arrangement. Project marginal operating cost and onboarding effort using these changes, including the burden placed on the current team.

A strong first group may be unusually motivated, well connected, or willing to tolerate manual assistance. Use a next cohort that reveals the intended expansion challenge, and provide the support a continuing service can afford. Learn whether the customer team can deploy and operate it through normal channels. A rollout sustained by constant field intervention has not yet established repeatability.

For account expansion, connect the next service to a buyer and benefit owner while preserving operator value. Demonstrate the useful capability and its operating implications with a relevant case. Internal referrals from a successful team can open the conversation; they do not establish fit or authorize a broader commercial promise. Recommend the next scope and commitment on its own economics.
